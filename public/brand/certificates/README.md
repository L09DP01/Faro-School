The logo and medal remain decorative artwork from the reference.

Built-in image_gen edit tool used (not CLI). Final prompt:

> Edit target: the supplied Faro School certificate. Produce its EMPTY PRINTING TEMPLATE. Preserve the original exact landscape aspect ratio, edge-to-edge composition and positions. This is a precise object removal task, NOT a redesign. Keep every decorative element unchanged: the entire navy/orange double-line stepped corner frame, tiny corner squares, orange curved hairline flourishes upper left and lower right, pale warm ivory laurel branches at both sides, large very faint graduation-cap watermark in center, the Faro School logo at top center, all orange horizontal diamond-centered dividers, the vertical lower separators and short orange underlines, and the navy/gold/orange medal with laurel wreath, ribbons, cap and white F at lower center. REMOVE all the OTHER text completely: certificate title, introduction, student name, completion sentence, course title, hours/instructor sentence, date label/date, certificate number, verification caption, signer name/title and legal footer. Also REMOVE the QR code completely (leave clean matching white paper there). Fill removed text seamlessly with its underlying subtle paper/watermark; do not leave ghost letters. Do NOT remove the FARO SCHOOL logo text or white F inside the medal. No added text or symbols. Match original ornaments exactly, no changes of scale, crop, arrangement, color or typography within retained logo/seal. Flat crisp high resolution printable 2D template, no mockup.

All certificate text is rendered separately from the shared French constants, using local OFL Tinos serif fonts and Inter. The footer remains editable. PDF and SVG use the same asset, typography, line positions and automatic fitting rules. The template alone is public, never a personalized PDF.
